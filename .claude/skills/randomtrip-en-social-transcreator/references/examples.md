# Examples — Spanish to English in Randomtrip's voice

Each pair below illustrates **transcreation**. Only the explicitly listed official brand lines in `SKILL.md` are locked. Other English examples are working translation guidance and can change with campaign context.

## 1. Brand manifesto-style introduction
**ES caption**
> No sabes adónde vas. Y eso es exactamente lo mejor. Somos Randomtrip. Diseñamos tu próximo viaje — vuelos, alojamiento — y te revelamos el destino solo 48 horas antes de salir. No es azar. Es serendipia diseñada. Anímate. El primer paso es elegir cuándo quieres viajar. Link en bio.

**EN (preferred for this exact post)**
> You don't know where you're going.
>
> And that's the best part.
>
> Meet Randomtrip.
>
> We design your trip — from flights to stays — and reveal your destination just 48 hours before you leave.
>
> Nothing left to chance. Plenty left to discover.
>
> Ready to let go of the plan?
>
> You choose when to travel. We'll take it from there.
>
> Find your Randomtrip. Link in bio.

**ES artwork** `SERENDIPIA DISEÑADA` → **EN artwork** `SERENDIPITY BY DESIGN.`

Why: the English doesn't mechanically render “Somos,” “No es azar,” and “Anímate.” It is still faithful to the particular post. **The flights/48-hour language is authorized by this example source only, not by generic brand identity.**

## 2. Planning-fatigue hook
**ES:** `MIENTRAS MÁS BUSCAS, MENOS ELIGES.` / `Y si esta vez eliges menos para descubrir más?`

**EN (on-image, option):**
> THE MORE YOU SEARCH, THE HARDER IT GETS TO CHOOSE.
>
> What if you made fewer decisions and discovered more?

Why: clear natural English beats mechanical `the more you look, the less you choose` in contexts where the source means choice overload. Shorten further for narrow overlays: `MORE TABS. LESS CLARITY.` — only if allowed to adapt more freely.

## 3. Contradictory reviews of famous landmarks
**ES direction:** A famous attraction has one-star and five-star reviews; the traveler is exhausted by opinions before leaving.

**EN candidate (short artwork):**
> FIVE STARS. ONE STAR.
> SAME LANDMARK.
> WE JUST WANTED A VACATION.

Why: comedic tension is the reviews, not that a hotel was unpleasant. **Do not fabricate a quotation as a real review** or pretend an invented rating belongs to a named landmark.

## 4. Curation and reassurance
**ES:** `NO LO ELEGISTE TÚ. TAMPOCO FUE AL AZAR.` / `Alguien miró más allá de lo obvio y encontró un lugar que vale la pena descubrir.`

**EN (artwork):**
> YOU DIDN'T PICK IT.
> IT WASN'T PICKED AT RANDOM, EITHER.
>
> Someone looked beyond the obvious and found a place worth discovering.

Why: don't undermine the brand icon by claiming the company literally “throws dice”; don't force the formal word `curation` into every line.

## 5. Destination reveal / FAQs
**ES:** `Y si no me gusta el destino?` / `Antes de viajar nos cuentas qué te gusta y qué no.`

**EN:**
> WHAT IF I DON'T LIKE WHERE I'M GOING?
>
> Before you go, tell us what you love — and what you'd rather avoid.

Why: answer the genuine concern in plain English, then explain the process as documented for the specific offer. Do **not** claim XSED has the same customization steps as other products.

## 6. The three ways to travel
**ES image:**
> LO INESPERADO SE VIVE A TU MANERA.
> SOLO. EN PAREJA. PARA ALGO ESPECIAL.
> Tú eliges con quién y la ocasión, el resto está por descubrir.

**EN working direction:**
> THE UNEXPECTED, YOUR WAY.
> SOLO. TOGETHER. FOR SOMETHING SPECIAL.
> You choose who you're going with — and what you're celebrating. The rest is yours to discover.

Why: make natural English rather than `you choose with whom and the occasion`. The heading `Together` is broader than `En pareja`; if the image must explicitly say couple, use `AS A COUPLE` rather than erasing the meaning. **Any final localized hero line requires brand approval if treated as a fixed campaign asset.**

## 7. Collection of Moments
**ES direction:** `Esta vez fue Brasil. Lugares que quizás nunca habrías buscado. Historias que valió la pena descubrir.`

**EN working caption:**
> This time, it was Brazil. 🇧🇷
>
> Places you might never have thought to look for. Stories worth coming home with.

Why: past tense, country as context, memories over destination list. Don't imply personal testimony or an actual completed trip if the post is purely speculative.

## 8. Product CTA: Sunday Drop
**ES:** `TU PRÓXIMO FIN DE SEMANA EMPIEZA ESTE DOMINGO.` / `4 horas para reservar.`

**EN (XSED only):**
> YOUR NEXT WEEKEND STARTS THIS SUNDAY.
>
> Four hours to book your getaway.

Why: keep timing and source scope; do not attach this line to general Randomtrip.

## 9. Long weekend (EXPLORA+ context)
**ES:** `Ese feriado largo que esta vez no vas a planear.`

**EN (working image line):**
> THAT LONG WEEKEND YOU'RE NOT PLANNING THIS TIME.

Or, if the art needs a shorter line:
> YOUR NEXT LONG WEEKEND. NO PLANNING REQUIRED.

Why: friendly tension, not a literal statement that every kind of trip has identical inclusions.

## 10. Comment replies (playful, insight-led)
**Incoming:** `Love this!` → **EN reply:** `Careful. You might end up loving a place you never would've picked. 👀`

**Incoming:** `This is genius!` → **EN reply:** `We figured your 27 open tabs deserved a day off.`

**Incoming:** `Where would I go?` → **EN reply:** `That's the part we're saving for later. The good kind of later. 😉`

**Incoming:** `I'm worried about the destination.` → **EN reply:** `Fair question. Unexpected doesn't mean random. Tell us what matters to you, and we'll explain how the trip is put together.` (Adjust to the correct product process; do not promise personalization where it doesn't apply.)

Why: every playful response connects either to planning fatigue or unexpected discovery. Customer-service questions get information, not teasing only.

## 11. Translation anti-patterns

| Don't ship | Better direction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `We are the surprise travel platform with personalized adventures.` | `Meet Randomtrip. The next place you love might be one you'd never choose.` | Promise beyond mechanics |
| `You should get out of your comfort zone to surprise yourself.` | `What if the next great story starts somewhere you didn't plan to go?` | Feels more human / less cliché |
| `Our specialists curate optimal itineraries.` | `Real people put the details together. You get to enjoy the discovery.` | Avoid platform jargon |
| `Let us roll the dice on your vacation.` | `Unexpected doesn't mean left to chance.` | Don't equate service with gambling or insult brand icon |
| `Enjoy the best surprise destination ever!` | `A place you never thought to look for. A story you won't forget.` | No hype, mystery-first selling or guarantee |
| `Don't worry, everything is included.` | `We take care of the planning.` | Don't add inclusions |
