# Translation glossary and brand lines

## Read this first
The phrases below come from different authority levels:
- **A · Approved English positioning:** wording explicitly present in final English positioning/manifesto or signature.
- **B · Strong recommended transcreation:** direction developed during the bilingual Instagram work or proposed here; **not** formally locked as a brand-wide English payoff.
- **C · Context-dependent glossary:** useful equivalents, not one-to-one mandates.

### A — canonical brand language

| Role | English wording | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Randomtrip | Consumer prose |
| Signature | `WONDER. WANDER. REPEAT.` | Exact brand sign-off / identity |
| North Star | `Wonder begins where certainty ends.` | High-level brand positioning, selected campaigns |
| Brand promise | `Trips you wouldn't have chosen—but end up loving.` | Promise positioning; not every caption |
| Brand essence | `Serendipity by Design` | Strategic / headline when it is the intended expression |
| Purpose | `Make travel feel magical again.` | Purpose, emotional work |
| Territory | `Discovery Beyond Choice` | Internal strategy only unless required |
| Big Creative Idea | `Wonder Once Again` | When this approved campaign line is being used |

### B — Spanish Brand Book payoffs: proposed English working versions

| Spanish official payoff | English *proposed* consumer expression | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| El asombro empieza donde termina la certeza. | `Wonder begins where certainty ends.` | **A — exact approved English North Star** |
| Menos elección. Más posibilidades. | `Fewer decisions. More possibilities.` | Preserve direct benefit, avoid “Less election.” Requires final copy lock |
| Hay viajes que nunca elegirías. Hasta que los vives. | `Some trips you'd never choose. Until you take them.` | Option when original two-beat creative rhythm is important. For **formal brand promise**, use approved “Trips you wouldn't have chosen—but end up loving.” |
| Vuelve a sentir la magia de viajar. | `Feel the magic of travel again.` | Consumer wording; related approved purpose is “Make travel feel magical again.” |
| No busques destinos. Encuentra historias. | `Don't chase destinations. Find stories.` | More idiomatic than “Don't search destinations.” Working version, not approved fixed payoff |

**Rule:** Do not insert these into a post if the original is not using them. A translator should not produce extra branded taglines to decorate posts.

### C — useful phrase glossary, not literal formulas

| Spanish | Useful English candidates | Avoid / notes |
|---|---|---|
| viaje | trip, journey, travel | “journey” can get grandiose; for product use “trip” |
| escapada | getaway, short escape, weekend getaway | “escape” can be poetic, “getaway” is idiomatic offer name |
| fin de semana | weekend | — |
| feriado largo / puente | long weekend, holiday weekend | Don't say “bank holiday” for global audience by default |
| descubrir lo inesperado | discover the unexpected, find what you wouldn't have looked for | Avoid using exactly same slogan constantly |
| dejar espacio para lo inesperado | leave room for the unexpected | Useful recurrent brand frame |
| menos elegir / más descubrir | fewer decisions / more discovery, choose less / discover more | Adapt to English rhythm |
| cansancio / fatiga de planificar | planning fatigue, tired of planning every last detail | “planning fatigue” is often an internal label; use human phrasing in captions |
| comparación / pestañas abiertas | comparing endlessly, too many open tabs | Use familiar digital-life humor |
| reseñas contradictorias | conflicting reviews, one-star and five-star reviews | Don't fabricate real star ratings or actual reviews |
| sin saber adónde vas | without knowing where you're headed / not knowing where you're going | Don't automatically call it a “mystery trip” |
| destino por descubrir | destination still under wraps / destination yet to be revealed | Adjust to brand warmth |
| curaduría humana | people behind every trip / thoughtfully picked by people who know | “human curation” can sound like agency jargon |
| cuidadosamente diseñado | thoughtfully designed, carefully put together | Avoid “bespoke” unless a specifically high-end offer supports it |
| nos encargamos del resto | we'll take it from here / we take care of the rest | Don't imply transport or everything included if not true |
| todo resuelto | the details are taken care of / we've got the planning covered | Better than “everything's included” |
| no es al azar | not random / not left to chance | Distinguish designed uncertainty from chance |
| tu historia | your story / the story you'll come back with | Brand focuses on stories, not bucket lists |
| descubrir tu Randomtrip | discover your Randomtrip / find your Randomtrip | CTA depending on actual funnel |
| link en la bio | link in bio | Preserve CTA mechanism |
| cupos limitados | limited spots / limited availability | Don't invent a quantity |
| se revela 48 h antes | revealed 48 hours before departure | Only for relevant offer or exact claim in input |
| salir de tu zona de confort | step outside your comfort zone | Don't overuse cliché; often express more freshly |
| magia de viajar | magic of travel / what makes travel feel magical | Avoid excessive “magical” adjectives |

## Keep these proper names unchanged
`Randomtrip`, `RANDOMTRIP`, `XSED`, `ESSENZA`, `MODO EXPLORA`, `EXPLORA+`, `BIVOUAC`, `ATELIER GETAWAY`, `Trippers`, `Sunday Drop`, `WONDER. WANDER. REPEAT.`

Where relevant, `X Suerte Es Domingo` / `Por suerte es domingo` is a named Spanish campaign expression; retain its name rather than assigning a new official English expansion. `Sunday Glitch` was used as XSED card language but is **not** a generic replacement for the XSED name.

## English locale and register
- Default **contemporary international / US English** for an international social audience; not a word-for-word copy of Spanish or ultra-American slang.
- Contractions are natural (`you'll`, `we'll`, `don't`).
- Use `you`, not formal `one`, unless a deliberate editorial tone demands it.
- Use `getaway`, `long weekend`, `travel`, `place`, `story`, `moment`, `discovery` where appropriate.
- Avoid `vacay`, `epic adventure`, `bucket-list worthy`, `hidden gem` spam, `wanderlust` filler and strings of superlatives.
- Don't automatically title-case every line; all caps is for graphical headlines, not every caption.
