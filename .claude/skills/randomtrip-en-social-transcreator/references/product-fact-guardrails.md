# Product facts and boundaries — preserve, never invent

## Translation versus product verification
This is a **language skill**, not a live product catalogue. Use the original post and the latest product brief or website copy supplied with each job as the source of truth for factual details. Product screenshots and earlier campaign discussions illustrate differences but may be superseded; do not turn them into unconditional claims.

### Scope: brand vs. offer
- **Randomtrip** is the overall brand/platform, with multiple travel experiences and traveler types.
- **XSED** is one specific entry product (Sunday Drop), **not the definition of all Randomtrip**.
- Also keep named offers `ESSENZA`, `MODO EXPLORA`, `EXPLORA+`, `BIVOUAC` and `ATELIER GETAWAY` distinct. Do not translate their names or collapse them all into XSED.
- Travelers may appear as `SOLUM`, `BOND`, `CREW` in some visual campaigns: **preserve product/experience persona names exactly as supplied**, along with a supplied © or ® only when actually present; do not change legal markings or insert them when absent.

### XSED-specific information appearing in project material
- Named XSED / *X Suerte Es Domingo*, associated with a **Sunday Drop** buying window.
- A one-night weekend escape in the supplied source; Saturday departure, Sunday return.
- A dinner and a local experience are listed in the relevant material. Some recent visual cards also show Sunday breakfast. **Do not add or remove breakfast unless it is in the current post/product brief.**
- A nearby destination (under 300 km) is part of the XSED description; **not a universal limit for other Randomtrip packages**.
- Own car / transport not included is cited in a particular XSED visual card; preserve it if stated and do not imply transport is included.
- The Sunday Drop has been described as a 4-hour window; historically a launch referenced 10 spots per country. **These campaign-specific quantities must be reproduced only when explicitly present in the material being translated.**
- Destination revealed 48 hours before departure may be part of the offering; do not force this detail into posts that are not about reveal mechanics.

### Other offers — examples, not claims to automatically insert
- `ESSENZA` is described in supplied cards as a shorter two-night escape.
- `MODO EXPLORA` as three nights, active/flexible travel.
- `EXPLORA+` as four nights, including long-weekend contexts; destinations may range to **continental**, not necessarily a foreign country.
- `BIVOUAC` as five nights, with the potential for farther-away / **up to intercontinental** travel; **never translate “can be intercontinental” as “always intercontinental.”**
- `ATELIER GETAWAY` as a highly flexible, customized/co-created option in supplied materials; do not make other standard packages sound fully bespoke.

These summary points are **not a substitute for updated terms**. Exact number of nights, price, transport, region, inclusions, restrictions, refund rules, city of departure and other selling claims should be sourced from the current input.

### Words that can accidentally overpromise

| English phrase | Risk | Safer when details are unknown |
|---|---|---|
| “Everything is included.” | Suggests transport, meals and extras are included in every package | “We take care of the planning.” |
| “Fully personalized to you.” | Implies every package has full personalization | “Thoughtfully put together.” |
| “Fly to anywhere in the world.” | Implies flights and unrestricted worldwide reach | Preserve exact source range and modal qualifier |
| “All Randomtrips are within 300 km.” | Imposes XSED radius on brand | Keep radius only with XSED |
| “Only 10 spots every Sunday.” | Makes launch condition permanent | Quote only in active drop copy supplied |
| “Your destination is revealed 48 hours before departure.” | May be true only for defined offers/contexts | Use only when specified |
| “Guaranteed unforgettable.” | Implies an unjustifiable outcome | “A story you didn't see coming.” (when suitable) |
| “Perfect for everyone.” | Promises universal satisfaction | “Designed with care.” |

### Reliability and safety
- If a specific post mentions a price, preserve numeric amount, currency and “per person / starting at” qualifiers. No FX conversion unless commissioned.
- Keep local day/date/time and timezone meaning exactly as supplied. Do not silently convert or rewrite `Sunday at 5pm` into a different market time.
- Keep paid/ad compliance fields, age restrictions if provided, terms, promos, disclaimer placement and promo codes verbatim.
- Never promise safety guarantees, unlimited support, exact satisfaction, or specific unexpected outcomes without source evidence.
- If a source contradicts itself — e.g. transport included vs. not included, 1 vs. 2 nights, “intercontinental only” vs. “up to intercontinental” — flag the mismatch as `Needs confirmation` rather than guessing.
