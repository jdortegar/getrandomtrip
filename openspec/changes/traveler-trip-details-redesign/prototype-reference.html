Day by day

Your itinerary

The same route every traveler on this drop is following — mornings among the vines, evenings at altitude. Nothing to plan, just show up.

  1. 01 Saturday Aug 22

    Touchdown in Wine Country

    1. 12:45 PM

      Arrival

      Land at Aeropuerto El Plumerillo (MDZ). Your driver is waiting at arrivals with a sign — no line to stand in.

    2. 3:00 PM

      Check-in

      Settle into Hotel Cavas del Sol in Luján de Cuyo — your home base among the vines for the next two nights.

    3. 5:30 PM

      Welcome toast

      A sunset walk through the estate's Malbec rows, sparkling wine in hand as the Andes go pink.

    4. 8:30 PM

      Dinner

      Asado tasting menu at Puesto Malbec, paired course by course with the region's reserve blends.

  2. 02 Sunday Aug 23

    Into the Vines

    1. 9:00 AM

      Breakfast

      An Andean-inspired spread on the hotel terrace, Aconcagua on the horizon.

    2. 10:30 AM

      Winery tour

      Barrel-room tasting and a blending session at Bodega Piedra Infinita, guided by the estate's head winemaker.

    3. 1:30 PM

      Lunch

      A long-table lunch under the parral, nearly everything on the plate grown on the estate.

    4. 4:30 PM

      Free time

      Optional detour to the Potrerillos reservoir, or a siesta by the pool — your call, nothing booked.

    5. 9:00 PM

      Dinner

      Tasting menu at the hotel's own restaurant, built around high-altitude ingredients.

  3. 03 Monday Aug 24

    One Last Pour

    1. 9:30 AM

      Farewell breakfast

      Coffee and pastries with one last look at the Andes over the vineyard.

    2. 11:00 AM

      Check-out

      Bags down. Take one more stroll through the rows before the car arrives.

    3. 12:30 PM

      Departure transfer

      Private car back to Aeropuerto El Plumerillo, timed to your flight.
